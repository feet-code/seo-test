A checklist for freelancer client project handoff and offboarding checklists should prevent missing decisions, not merely prove that somebody clicked boxes. The checklist below is designed for freelancers and independent professional service businesses and centers on one result: **the client can operate the delivered work and locate every agreed artifact without depending on the freelancer's memory**.

## Before the work starts

- Confirm Client and project
- Confirm Deliverable
- Confirm Final file or system link
- Confirm Owner after handoff

Also name the owner and the expected completion condition. If either is unknown, the work is not ready to enter the active queue.

## While the work is moving

- Update Inventory deliverables and ownership
- Update Prepare files and secure access transfer
- Update Document operation and maintenance
- Update Review open items with the client
- Update Obtain acceptance and close

Every update should change a decision. Notes such as “followed up” are weak unless they also include the channel, result, next date, and owner.

## Before marking it complete

- Verify Access transfer status
- Verify Operating instruction
- Verify Open item
- Verify Acceptance criterion
- Verify Client acknowledgement

Confirm that the actual outcome—not just an activity—has been recorded. If the process ended early, use a closed reason rather than deleting the record.

## Copy-and-paste weekly review

- [ ] Review records where a deliverable lacks an agreed final format or owner
- [ ] Review records where access transfer is incomplete or insecure
- [ ] Review records where the client identifies an unresolved item during review

- [ ] Check for sending a folder with no index
- [ ] Check for sharing credentials in an unsafe channel
- [ ] Check for treating training as proof of client acceptance
- [ ] Check for closing before unresolved items have owners and dates

## Make the checklist measurable

Choose one metric before the next cycle. Good options for this workflow are First-pass acceptance rate, Post-handoff clarification count, Open-item closure time. A checklist that never changes a metric or prevents a known failure mode is probably administrative overhead.

## Assign ownership and escalation

Put one role—not a group—next to every item that can remain open. Define a backup owner and an escalation time for work that affects a customer, client, participant, or delivery promise. During review, separate **not started**, **waiting on someone**, and **failed validation**; those states need different actions. If a checklist item repeatedly waits on the same dependency, redesign the intake or handoff instead of adding more reminder boxes.
