The most useful freelancer client project handoff and offboarding checklists template is a small operating record. It should answer what is happening, who owns it, what evidence exists, and when the next decision occurs. This structure works in a spreadsheet, database, or focused application.

## Suggested statuses

Use workflow statuses that describe reality: **Inventory Deliverables And Ownership → Prepare Files And Secure Access Transfer → Document Operation And Maintenance → Review Open Items With The Client → Obtain Acceptance And Close**. Add **Waiting** only when you also capture a waiting reason and review date. Add **Closed—Not Completed** when an item legitimately ends without the desired outcome.

## Follow-up rules

- When a deliverable lacks an agreed final format or owner, assign a next action and review date.
- When access transfer is incomplete or insecure, assign a next action and review date.
- When the client identifies an unresolved item during review, assign a next action and review date.

Avoid reminders with no stop condition. A rule should say when it starts, who receives it, what counts as a response, and when a person should take over.

## Example records

- A website launches but analytics ownership remains on the freelancer's account
- Final design files are present but fonts and licenses are undocumented
- The client accepts delivery while one content item has a named post-launch date

For each example, write the current status, next action, owner, and supporting evidence. This makes the template testable with real work rather than idealized sample data.

## Quality-control rules

- The handoff has a human-readable index
- Access transfer follows appropriate security practice
- Acceptance criteria come from the agreed scope
- Open items have owners, dates, and support boundaries

Before adding automation, run the template manually for a week. Remove ambiguous fields and confirm that two different users classify the same situation the same way. Consistency matters more than having a long form.
