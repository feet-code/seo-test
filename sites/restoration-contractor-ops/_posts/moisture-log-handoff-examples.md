Examples make water restoration moisture log handoff easier to design because they reveal where a neat diagram meets messy work. The scenarios below are not claims about a particular company; they are test cases small water, fire, and property-restoration contractors can run against a template or software trial.

### Scenario 1: A wall-cavity reading lacks the exact elevation

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Job, structure, and affected area, Visit date, technician, and conditions, Material and exact reading location, then move it through load the affected-area and equipment plan and capture readings with location and method. If a visit ends with required evidence missing, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason. ### Scenario 2: An air mover is removed but the prior placement photo is missing

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Visit date, technician, and conditions, Material and exact reading location, Instrument and reading, then move it through load the affected-area and equipment plan and capture readings with location and method. If readings or material condition change the drying plan, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason. ### Scenario 3: A weekend technician inherits a commercial job from the weekday crew

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Material and exact reading location, Instrument and reading, Photo and annotation, then move it through load the affected-area and equipment plan and capture readings with location and method. If a new technician takes over the next visit, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason.

## Debrief each scenario

After running a scenario, ask:

- Did the record make every open drying documentation handoff needs one owner and a next review time?
- Did the record make completion requires recorded evidence that every drying visit produces a time-stamped, location-specific record that supports the next field decision and controlled job documentation?
- Did the record make automated reminders stop after verified completion or a documented closed reason?
- Did the record make keep job file, field-documentation, estimating, and carrier systems as the system of record; only necessary coordination data belongs here?

Also check whether a new teammate could identify the owner, next action, and finish condition without opening another system.

## Convert scenarios into acceptance tests

Use the normal case, waiting case, and closed-without-completion case in every software demo. Require the vendor—or your own prototype—to show the full workflow rather than isolated feature screens. Export the resulting records and verify that the status history remains understandable.
