Remakes are opened as replacement cases while fit issue, intake quality, prescription change, material, design, production step, shipping damage, practice handling, and disposition remain unstructured. For independent dental laboratories serving local dental practices, the useful goal is not to add another dashboard. It is to create a small, visible process that produces this outcome: **every remake receives a respectful evidence-based operational review, explicit responsibility and commercial treatment, and a prevention action when warranted**.

## Define the finish line first

Write the outcome in operational terms. A teammate should be able to look at one record and tell what happened, who owns the next action, and what is blocking completion. Use these rules as the boundary:

- Every open remake review needs one owner and a next review time
- Completion requires recorded evidence that every remake receives a respectful evidence-based operational review, explicit responsibility and commercial treatment, and a prevention action when warranted
- Automated reminders stop after verified completion or a documented closed reason
- Keep the dental-lab case, prescription, scan, file, production, shipping, and billing platform as the system of record; only necessary coordination data belongs here

## A practical end-to-end workflow

### 1. Register remake against the original case

Record **Practice original and remake cases** at this point, name the person responsible, and define the evidence that allows the work to move to the next step. The exit condition should be observable: either the team can collect practice report and returned evidence, or the record remains open with a reason and next action.

### 2. Collect practice report and returned evidence

Record **Reported issue date and affected unit** at this point, name the person responsible, and define the evidence that allows the work to move to the next step. The exit condition should be observable: either the team can review intake design production and delivery history, or the record remains open with a reason and next action.

### 3. Review intake design production and delivery history

Record **Practice observations photos and return status** at this point, name the person responsible, and define the evidence that allows the work to move to the next step. The exit condition should be observable: either the team can decide remake scope priority and commercial handling, or the record remains open with a reason and next action.

### 4. Decide remake scope priority and commercial handling

Record **Original prescription files and approvals** at this point, name the person responsible, and define the evidence that allows the work to move to the next step. The exit condition should be observable: either the team can close after replacement outcome and prevention review, or the record remains open with a reason and next action.

### 5. Close after replacement outcome and prevention review

Record **Production checkpoints materials and technicians** at this point, name the person responsible, and define the evidence that allows the work to move to the next step. The exit condition should be observable: either the team can close the record and review the outcome, or the record remains open with a reason and next action.

## Handle exceptions without hiding them

The process needs an explicit waiting state. Do not mark work complete because a reminder was sent or a form was opened. Keep the record open until the real exit condition is met. These events deserve a named exception path:

- a practice requests correction replacement or remake
- returned evidence conflicts with the original record
- review identifies a repeated preventable failure mode

For each exception, store the reason, the next review date, and the person who can unblock it. That makes a weekly review useful instead of turning it into a search across email, chat, and spreadsheets.

## Start with one live cycle

Run the workflow for one client, location, role, order, or participant before standardizing it. At the end, remove fields nobody used, add evidence that was missing, and keep the status list short. The workflow is ready to scale when another person can operate it without asking the original owner what each row means.
