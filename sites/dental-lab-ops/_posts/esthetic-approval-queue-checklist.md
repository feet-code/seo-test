A checklist for dental lab shade and design approval tracking should prevent missing decisions, not merely prove that somebody clicked boxes. The checklist below is designed for independent dental laboratories serving local dental practices and centers on one result: **every requested esthetic or design decision is tied to a specific review artifact, authorized practice response, effective version, and production release**.

## Before the work starts

- Confirm Practice case and patient reference
- Confirm Decision type and clinical owner
- Confirm Artifact file image or design version
- Confirm Question options and response deadline

Also name the owner and the expected completion condition. If either is unknown, the work is not ready to enter the active queue.

## While the work is moving

- Update Prepare the review artifact and decision question
- Update Send it through the approved practice channel
- Update Record response clarification or requested change
- Update Publish the accepted version to production
- Update Verify downstream work uses that release

Every update should change a decision. Notes such as “followed up” are weak unless they also include the channel, result, next date, and owner.

## Before marking it complete

- Verify Practice response responder and time
- Verify Requested change and revised version
- Verify Lab reviewer and production release
- Verify Technician acknowledgment and superseded assets

Confirm that the actual outcome—not just an activity—has been recorded. If the process ended early, use a closed reason rather than deleting the record.

## Copy-and-paste weekly review

- [ ] Review records where a case requires shade design or try-in feedback
- [ ] Review records where the practice requests a change or clarification
- [ ] Review records where production cannot identify the current approved version

- [ ] Check for asking approve without identifying the artifact version
- [ ] Check for treating patient-facing feedback as the prescribing practice's authorization
- [ ] Check for leaving rejected files available to production
- [ ] Check for continuing work while a requested clarification is open

## Make the checklist measurable

Choose one metric before the next cycle. Good options for this workflow are Decision cycle time, Revision loops, Wrong-version incident. A checklist that never changes a metric or prevents a known failure mode is probably administrative overhead.

## Assign ownership and escalation

Put one role—not a group—next to every item that can remain open. Define a backup owner and an escalation time for work that affects a customer, client, participant, or delivery promise. During review, separate **not started**, **waiting on someone**, and **failed validation**; those states need different actions. If a checklist item repeatedly waits on the same dependency, redesign the intake or handoff instead of adding more reminder boxes.
