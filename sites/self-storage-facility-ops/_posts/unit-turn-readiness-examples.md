Examples make self-storage move-out inspection and unit turn tracking easier to design because they reveal where a neat diagram meets messy work. The scenarios below are not claims about a particular company; they are test cases independent self-storage facilities and small multi-site operators can run against a template or software trial.

### Scenario 1: A unit is empty but the lock and access record remain active

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Facility and unit, Tenant move-out and key or access return, Inspection time and inspector, then move it through confirm tenant move-out and possession and inspect condition and capture evidence. If a tenant reports move-out or access ends, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason. ### Scenario 2: Inspection finds shelving that must be removed

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Tenant move-out and key or access return, Inspection time and inspector, Condition photos and findings, then move it through confirm tenant move-out and possession and inspect condition and capture evidence. If inspection finds damage, property, or unresolved access, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason. ### Scenario 3: Cleaning finishes Friday but web availability still shows occupied

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Inspection time and inspector, Condition photos and findings, Cleaning or repair tasks, then move it through confirm tenant move-out and possession and inspect condition and capture evidence. If all work closes but the unit is not yet available online, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason.

## Debrief each scenario

After running a scenario, ask:

- Did the record make every open unit-turn task needs one owner and a next review time?
- Did the record make completion requires recorded evidence that every vacated unit is inspected, cleared, reconciled, and published as rentable or held with a named reason?
- Did the record make automated reminders stop after verified completion or a documented closed reason?
- Did the record make keep the facility-management, access, lease, and payment platform as the system of record; only necessary coordination data belongs here?

Also check whether a new teammate could identify the owner, next action, and finish condition without opening another system.

## Convert scenarios into acceptance tests

Use the normal case, waiting case, and closed-without-completion case in every software demo. Require the vendor—or your own prototype—to show the full workflow rather than isolated feature screens. Export the resulting records and verify that the status history remains understandable.
