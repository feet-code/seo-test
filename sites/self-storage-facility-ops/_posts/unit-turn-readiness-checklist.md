A checklist for self-storage move-out inspection and unit turn tracking should prevent missing decisions, not merely prove that somebody clicked boxes. The checklist below is designed for independent self-storage facilities and small multi-site operators and centers on one result: **every vacated unit is inspected, cleared, reconciled, and published as rentable or held with a named reason**.

## Before the work starts

- Confirm Facility and unit
- Confirm Tenant move-out and key or access return
- Confirm Inspection time and inspector
- Confirm Condition photos and findings

Also name the owner and the expected completion condition. If either is unknown, the work is not ready to enter the active queue.

## While the work is moving

- Update Confirm tenant move-out and possession
- Update Inspect condition and capture evidence
- Update Assign cleaning, repair, or removal work
- Update Reconcile charges, access, and unit status
- Update Verify readiness and publish availability

Every update should change a decision. Notes such as “followed up” are weak unless they also include the channel, result, next date, and owner.

## Before marking it complete

- Verify Cleaning or repair tasks
- Verify Property-left-behind decision
- Verify Final account and access status
- Verify Rentable time or hold reason

Confirm that the actual outcome—not just an activity—has been recorded. If the process ended early, use a closed reason rather than deleting the record.

## Copy-and-paste weekly review

- [ ] Review records where a tenant reports move-out or access ends
- [ ] Review records where inspection finds damage, property, or unresolved access
- [ ] Review records where all work closes but the unit is not yet available online

- [ ] Check for marking vacant before confirming possession
- [ ] Check for cleaning before condition evidence is captured
- [ ] Check for publishing availability while repair work remains open
- [ ] Check for closing the account without updating gate or unit status

## Make the checklist measurable

Choose one metric before the next cycle. Good options for this workflow are Vacant-to-rentable time, First-inspection completeness, Availability correction rate. A checklist that never changes a metric or prevents a known failure mode is probably administrative overhead.

## Assign ownership and escalation

Put one role—not a group—next to every item that can remain open. Define a backup owner and an escalation time for work that affects a customer, client, participant, or delivery promise. During review, separate **not started**, **waiting on someone**, and **failed validation**; those states need different actions. If a checklist item repeatedly waits on the same dependency, redesign the intake or handoff instead of adding more reminder boxes.
