Examples make interview feedback collection and hiring debrief workflows easier to design because they reveal where a neat diagram meets messy work. The scenarios below are not claims about a particular company; they are test cases independent recruiters and boutique recruiting firms can run against a template or software trial.

### Scenario 1: A technical interviewer rates low confidence but provides no observed evidence

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Candidate and stage, Interviewer, Evaluation area, then move it through assign evaluation areas and collect independent feedback. If an interviewer has not submitted before the debrief cutoff, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason. ### Scenario 2: A panel splits on client-facing ability and needs one targeted follow-up

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Interviewer, Evaluation area, Evidence observed, then move it through assign evaluation areas and collect independent feedback. If two evaluations conflict on the same criterion, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason. ### Scenario 3: The final interviewer submits notes after the recruiter already promised an update

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Evaluation area, Evidence observed, Concern, then move it through assign evaluation areas and collect independent feedback. If a concern has no supporting example or follow-up question, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason.

## Debrief each scenario

After running a scenario, ask:

- Did the record make independent feedback is captured before group discussion?
- Did the record make evidence is separated from conclusions?
- Did the record make missing feedback has a cutoff and escalation owner?
- Did the record make the final decision includes the candidate communication owner?

Also check whether a new teammate could identify the owner, next action, and finish condition without opening another system.

## Convert scenarios into acceptance tests

Use the normal case, waiting case, and closed-without-completion case in every software demo. Require the vendor—or your own prototype—to show the full workflow rather than isolated feature screens. Export the resulting records and verify that the status history remains understandable.
