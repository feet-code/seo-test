Examples make candidate follow-up tracking for recruiting agencies easier to design because they reveal where a neat diagram meets messy work. The scenarios below are not claims about a particular company; they are test cases independent recruiters and boutique recruiting firms can run against a template or software trial.

### Scenario 1: A shortlisted candidate was promised feedback Friday but the client has not responded

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Candidate and role, Last meaningful contact, Promised update, then move it through capture the communication promise and set the next-contact date. If a promised update date passes without a logged message, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason. ### Scenario 2: A passive candidate asks to reconnect after a compensation review

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Last meaningful contact, Promised update, Next-contact date, then move it through capture the communication promise and set the next-contact date. If a candidate replies with a question or changed availability, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason. ### Scenario 3: A recruiter leaves and twenty active conversations need a new owner

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Promised update, Next-contact date, Channel, then move it through capture the communication promise and set the next-contact date. If a role is paused, filled, or reassigned, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason.

## Debrief each scenario

After running a scenario, ask:

- Did the record make a reminder must stop when the candidate or role closes?
- Did the record make every message should communicate a real status or next decision?
- Did the record make the ats remains the candidate system of record?
- Did the record make sensitive candidate details should not be copied into unnecessary tools?

Also check whether a new teammate could identify the owner, next action, and finish condition without opening another system.

## Convert scenarios into acceptance tests

Use the normal case, waiting case, and closed-without-completion case in every software demo. Require the vendor—or your own prototype—to show the full workflow rather than isolated feature screens. Export the resulting records and verify that the status history remains understandable.
