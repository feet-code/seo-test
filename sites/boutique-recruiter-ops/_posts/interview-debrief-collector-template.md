The most useful interview feedback collection and hiring debrief workflows template is a small operating record. It should answer what is happening, who owns it, what evidence exists, and when the next decision occurs. This structure works in a spreadsheet, database, or focused application.

## Recommended record fields

| Field | Why it exists | Update point |
|---|---|---|
| Candidate and stage | Prevents the record from depending on memory or an inbox search | Assign evaluation areas |
| Interviewer | Prevents the record from depending on memory or an inbox search | Collect independent feedback |

## Suggested statuses

Use workflow statuses that describe reality: **Assign Evaluation Areas → Collect Independent Feedback → Flag Missing Evidence → Run The Debrief → Record The Decision And Candidate Update**. Add **Waiting** only when you also capture a waiting reason and review date. Add **Closed—Not Completed** when an item legitimately ends without the desired outcome.

## Follow-up rules

- When an interviewer has not submitted before the debrief cutoff, assign a next action and review date.
- When two evaluations conflict on the same criterion, assign a next action and review date.
- When a concern has no supporting example or follow-up question, assign a next action and review date.

Avoid reminders with no stop condition. A rule should say when it starts, who receives it, what counts as a response, and when a person should take over.

## Example records

- A technical interviewer rates low confidence but provides no observed evidence
- A panel splits on client-facing ability and needs one targeted follow-up
- The final interviewer submits notes after the recruiter already promised an update

For each example, write the current status, next action, owner, and supporting evidence. This makes the template testable with real work rather than idealized sample data.

## Quality-control rules

- Independent feedback is captured before group discussion
- Evidence is separated from conclusions
- Missing feedback has a cutoff and escalation owner
- The final decision includes the candidate communication owner

Before adding automation, run the template manually for a week. Remove ambiguous fields and confirm that two different users classify the same situation the same way. Consistency matters more than having a long form.
