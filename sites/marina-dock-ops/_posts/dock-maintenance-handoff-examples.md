Examples make marina dock maintenance handoff easier to design because they reveal where a neat diagram meets messy work. The scenarios below are not claims about a particular company; they are test cases independent marinas, yacht clubs, and small dock operations can run against a template or software trial.

### Scenario 1: A power pedestal trips for two occupied slips

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Marina, dock, slip, and asset, Reported time and source, Issue and impact, then move it through capture the issue and exact dock location and assess impact and contain affected access or service. If a dock or boater reports a facility issue, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason. ### Scenario 2: A damaged finger pier requires temporary access closure

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Reported time and source, Issue and impact, Containment and affected slips, then move it through capture the issue and exact dock location and assess impact and contain affected access or service. If repair timing or impact changes affected slip availability, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason. ### Scenario 3: A contractor replaces a water valve but the leak persists on inspection

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Issue and impact, Containment and affected slips, Owner, contractor, and access plan, then move it through capture the issue and exact dock location and assess impact and contain affected access or service. If contractor completion fails marina inspection, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason.

## Debrief each scenario

After running a scenario, ask:

- Did the record make every open dock maintenance issue needs one owner and a next review time?
- Did the record make completion requires recorded evidence that every marina maintenance issue has contained impact, assigned repair, affected-slip communication, and verified return to service?
- Did the record make automated reminders stop after verified completion or a documented closed reason?
- Did the record make keep the slip, reservation, boater, billing, utility, and maintenance platform as the system of record; only necessary coordination data belongs here?

Also check whether a new teammate could identify the owner, next action, and finish condition without opening another system.

## Convert scenarios into acceptance tests

Use the normal case, waiting case, and closed-without-completion case in every software demo. Require the vendor—or your own prototype—to show the full workflow rather than isolated feature screens. Export the resulting records and verify that the status history remains understandable.
