The most useful community theater rehearsal conflict tracking template is a small operating record. It should answer what is happening, who owns it, what evidence exists, and when the next decision occurs. This structure works in a spreadsheet, database, or focused application.

## Recommended record fields

| Field | Why it exists | Update point |
|---|---|---|
| Production rehearsal and schedule version | Prevents the record from depending on memory or an inbox search | Register the conflict against the current schedule |

## Suggested statuses

Use workflow statuses that describe reality: **Register The Conflict Against The Current Schedule → Identify Scenes Roles Staff And Rooms Affected → Compare Approved Resolution Options → Publish The Revised Call And Supersede Old Versions → Collect Acknowledgments And Handle Remaining Exceptions**. Add **Waiting** only when you also capture a waiting reason and review date. Add **Closed—Not Completed** when an item legitimately ends without the desired outcome.

## Follow-up rules

- When a cast crew room or director conflict is reported, assign a next action and review date.
- When a resolution changes another role or scene call, assign a next action and review date.
- When an affected participant misses the acknowledgment cutoff, assign a next action and review date.

Avoid reminders with no stop condition. A rule should say when it starts, who receives it, what counts as a response, and when a person should take over.

## Example records

- A lead becomes unavailable for a blocking rehearsal
- The stage is lost to another event
- A fight call requires participants not on the original schedule

For each example, write the current status, next action, owner, and supporting evidence. This makes the template testable with real work rather than idealized sample data.

## Quality-control rules

- Every open rehearsal conflict needs one owner and a next review time
- Completion requires recorded evidence that every material rehearsal conflict is resolved against scene and role dependencies, published as one current schedule, and acknowledged by affected participants
- Automated reminders stop after verified completion or a documented closed reason
- Keep the theater audition, cast, rehearsal, scene, volunteer, inventory, and production platform as the system of record; only necessary coordination data belongs here

Before adding automation, run the template manually for a week. Remove ambiguous fields and confirm that two different users classify the same situation the same way. Consistency matters more than having a long form.
