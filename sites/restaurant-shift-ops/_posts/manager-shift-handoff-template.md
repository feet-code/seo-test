The most useful restaurant manager shift handoff tracking template is a small operating record. It should answer what is happening, who owns it, what evidence exists, and when the next decision occurs. This structure works in a spreadsheet, database, or focused application.

## Recommended record fields

| Field | Why it exists | Update point |

## Suggested statuses

Use workflow statuses that describe reality: **Capture The Unresolved Issue During The Shift → Classify Impact And Immediate Containment → Assign The Next Action And Due Time → Review And Accept At Manager Handoff → Resolve Escalate Or Carry Forward With Evidence**. Add **Waiting** only when you also capture a waiting reason and review date. Add **Closed—Not Completed** when an item legitimately ends without the desired outcome.

## Follow-up rules

- When a shift ends with unresolved work, assign a next action and review date.
- When an issue affects the next shift's service or staffing, assign a next action and review date.
- When a promised update or vendor response becomes overdue, assign a next action and review date.

Avoid reminders with no stop condition. A rule should say when it starts, who receives it, what counts as a response, and when a person should take over.

## Example records

- A guest recovery call is due tomorrow morning
- The ice machine repair has a vendor ETA but no verification
- A staffing callout remains uncovered for brunch

For each example, write the current status, next action, owner, and supporting evidence. This makes the template testable with real work rather than idealized sample data.

## Quality-control rules

- Every open shift issue needs one owner and a next review time
- Completion requires recorded evidence that every unresolved shift issue transfers with impact, owner, next action, due time, and explicit acceptance by the next manager
- Automated reminders stop after verified completion or a documented closed reason
- Keep the POS, inventory, recipe, scheduling, and maintenance systems as the system of record; only necessary coordination data belongs here

Before adding automation, run the template manually for a week. Remove ambiguous fields and confirm that two different users classify the same situation the same way. Consistency matters more than having a long form.
