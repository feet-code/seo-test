A checklist for environmental sampling event readiness should prevent missing decisions, not merely prove that somebody clicked boxes. The checklist below is designed for small environmental consulting and field-sampling teams and centers on one result: **every sampling event is released by a qualified reviewer with current plan, locations, equipment, containers, laboratory coordination, access, and safety prerequisites**.

## Before the work starts

- Confirm Project event and plan version
- Confirm Locations matrices methods and sample IDs
- Confirm Containers preservatives labels and blanks
- Confirm Equipment calibration and consumables

Also name the owner and the expected completion condition. If either is unknown, the work is not ready to enter the active queue.

## While the work is moving

- Update Load the approved sampling plan and event scope
- Update Build bottle equipment label and calibration needs
- Update Confirm access safety laboratory and courier timing
- Update Resolve readiness exceptions through qualified staff
- Update Release the versioned field packet and verify receipt

Every update should change a decision. Notes such as “followed up” are weak unless they also include the channel, result, next date, and owner.

## Before marking it complete

- Verify Access utility weather and safety plan
- Verify Laboratory bottle receipt and hold-time coordination
- Verify Courier cooler and shipping plan
- Verify Qualified reviewer release and team acknowledgment

Confirm that the actual outcome—not just an activity—has been recorded. If the process ended early, use a closed reason rather than deleting the record.

## Copy-and-paste weekly review

- [ ] Review records where a sampling event enters the mobilization window
- [ ] Review records where plan access lab equipment or weather status changes
- [ ] Review records where the field team finds a prerequisite conflict

- [ ] Check for copying last event without checking plan revision
- [ ] Check for treating available bottles as method-compatible
- [ ] Check for automating a method or safety decision without qualified review
- [ ] Check for releasing while laboratory receipt timing is unconfirmed

## Make the checklist measurable

Choose one metric before the next cycle. Good options for this workflow are Ready-by-mobilization rate, Field deviation rate, Unused or missing container variance. A checklist that never changes a metric or prevents a known failure mode is probably administrative overhead.

## Assign ownership and escalation

Put one role—not a group—next to every item that can remain open. Define a backup owner and an escalation time for work that affects a customer, client, participant, or delivery promise. During review, separate **not started**, **waiting on someone**, and **failed validation**; those states need different actions. If a checklist item repeatedly waits on the same dependency, redesign the intake or handoff instead of adding more reminder boxes.
