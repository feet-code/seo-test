Examples make hotel guest maintenance handoff easier to design because they reveal where a neat diagram meets messy work. The scenarios below are not claims about a particular company; they are test cases independent boutique hotels and small hospitality teams can run against a template or software trial.

### Scenario 1: A shower drain backs up while the guest is at breakfast

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Guest, stay, and room, Issue and reported time, Impact and urgency, then move it through capture the issue and guest impact and triage urgency, room status, and access. If an in-house guest reports a room defect, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason. ### Scenario 2: An HVAC part delay requires a room move

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Issue and reported time, Impact and urgency, Permission and access window, then move it through capture the issue and guest impact and triage urgency, room status, and access. If repair cannot meet the communicated update or requires a room move, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason. ### Scenario 3: Engineering resets a television but the remote still fails during verification

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Impact and urgency, Permission and access window, Owner, vendor, and next update, then move it through capture the issue and guest impact and triage urgency, room status, and access. If the technician closes work but room verification fails, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason.

## Debrief each scenario

After running a scenario, ask:

- Did the record make every open guest maintenance issue needs one owner and a next review time?
- Did the record make completion requires recorded evidence that every guest-impacting maintenance issue has a coordinated access plan, verified repair, and completed guest follow-up?
- Did the record make automated reminders stop after verified completion or a documented closed reason?
- Did the record make keep pms, room-status, maintenance, and guest-service systems as the system of record; only necessary coordination data belongs here?

Also check whether a new teammate could identify the owner, next action, and finish condition without opening another system.

## Convert scenarios into acceptance tests

Use the normal case, waiting case, and closed-without-completion case in every software demo. Require the vendor—or your own prototype—to show the full workflow rather than isolated feature screens. Export the resulting records and verify that the status history remains understandable.
