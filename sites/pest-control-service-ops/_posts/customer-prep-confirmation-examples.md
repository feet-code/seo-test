Examples make pest control service preparation confirmation easier to design because they reveal where a neat diagram meets messy work. The scenarios below are not claims about a particular company; they are test cases independent pest control companies and small recurring-service teams can run against a template or software trial.

### Scenario 1: A kitchen treatment requires counters cleared

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Customer property and service, Treatment type and target area, Preparation checklist version, then move it through create requirements from service type and property and send plain-language preparation instructions. If a preparation-required service is booked, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason. ### Scenario 2: A resident cannot relocate a pet during the window

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Treatment type and target area, Preparation checklist version, Required-by and visit window, then move it through create requirements from service type and property and send plain-language preparation instructions. If the customer reports an unmet requirement, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason. ### Scenario 3: An apartment contact confirms unit access but not common-room access

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Preparation checklist version, Required-by and visit window, Delivery channel and evidence, then move it through create requirements from service type and property and send plain-language preparation instructions. If the visit time or treatment scope changes, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason.

## Debrief each scenario

After running a scenario, ask:

- Did the record make every open service preparation record needs one owner and a next review time?
- Did the record make completion requires recorded evidence that every treatment starts with the required customer preparation confirmed or a documented service decision before technician arrival?
- Did the record make automated reminders stop after verified completion or a documented closed reason?
- Did the record make keep the pest-control crm, route, service-history, chemical-use, and billing platform as the system of record; only necessary coordination data belongs here?

Also check whether a new teammate could identify the owner, next action, and finish condition without opening another system.

## Convert scenarios into acceptance tests

Use the normal case, waiting case, and closed-without-completion case in every software demo. Require the vendor—or your own prototype—to show the full workflow rather than isolated feature screens. Export the resulting records and verify that the status history remains understandable.
