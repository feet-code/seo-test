Examples make fitness studio trial follow-up easier to design because they reveal where a neat diagram meets messy work. The scenarios below are not claims about a particular company; they are test cases boutique fitness studios and group-class operators can run against a template or software trial.

### Scenario 1: A first-time Pilates visitor asks about beginner times

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Prospect and preferred channel, Lead source, Goal and class interest, then move it through capture the trial source and goal and confirm attendance and class experience. If an attended trial has no follow-up by the target time, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. ## Debrief each scenario

After running a scenario, ask:

- Did the record make every open trial-member opportunity needs one owner and a next review time?
- Did the record make completion requires recorded evidence that every attended trial receives one relevant membership next step or a documented no-contact outcome?
- Did the record make automated reminders stop after verified completion or a documented closed reason?
- Did the record make keep studio booking and membership platform as the system of record; only necessary coordination data belongs here?

Also check whether a new teammate could identify the owner, next action, and finish condition without opening another system.

## Convert scenarios into acceptance tests

Use the normal case, waiting case, and closed-without-completion case in every software demo. Require the vendor—or your own prototype—to show the full workflow rather than isolated feature screens. Export the resulting records and verify that the status history remains understandable.
