Examples make fitness instructor substitution tracking easier to design because they reveal where a neat diagram meets messy work. The scenarios below are not claims about a particular company; they are test cases boutique fitness studios and group-class operators can run against a template or software trial.

### Scenario 1: A cycling instructor calls out two hours before class

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Class, location, and time, Absent instructor and reason category, Required qualification, then move it through open the absence against the scheduled class and identify eligible available substitutes. If an instructor reports an absence, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason. ### Scenario 2: A substitute can teach yoga but not the scheduled advanced format

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Absent instructor and reason category, Required qualification, Candidate substitutes, then move it through open the absence against the scheduled class and identify eligible available substitutes. If no eligible substitute accepts by the escalation time, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason. ### Scenario 3: Coverage is confirmed but the studio door code changed

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Required qualification, Candidate substitutes, Confirmed substitute, then move it through open the absence against the scheduled class and identify eligible available substitutes. If a confirmed substitute withdraws or lacks access, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason.

## Debrief each scenario

After running a scenario, ask:

- Did the record make every open class coverage exception needs one owner and a next review time?
- Did the record make completion requires recorded evidence that every instructor absence is covered by an eligible substitute or escalated to a documented class change before members arrive?
- Did the record make automated reminders stop after verified completion or a documented closed reason?
- Did the record make keep studio booking and membership platform as the system of record; only necessary coordination data belongs here?

Also check whether a new teammate could identify the owner, next action, and finish condition without opening another system.

## Convert scenarios into acceptance tests

Use the normal case, waiting case, and closed-without-completion case in every software demo. Require the vendor—or your own prototype—to show the full workflow rather than isolated feature screens. Export the resulting records and verify that the status history remains understandable.
