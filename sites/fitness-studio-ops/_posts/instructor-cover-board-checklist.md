A checklist for fitness instructor substitution tracking should prevent missing decisions, not merely prove that somebody clicked boxes. The checklist below is designed for boutique fitness studios and group-class operators and centers on one result: **every instructor absence is covered by an eligible substitute or escalated to a documented class change before members arrive**.

## Before the work starts

- Confirm Class, location, and time
- Confirm Absent instructor and reason category
- Confirm Required qualification
- Confirm Candidate substitutes

Also name the owner and the expected completion condition. If either is unknown, the work is not ready to enter the active queue.

## While the work is moving

- Update Open the absence against the scheduled class
- Update Identify eligible available substitutes
- Update Confirm coverage and compensation
- Update Transfer class and facility instructions
- Update Publish the schedule change and verify delivery

Every update should change a decision. Notes such as “followed up” are weak unless they also include the channel, result, next date, and owner.

## Before marking it complete

- Verify Confirmed substitute
- Verify Pay or credit adjustment
- Verify Access and class notes
- Verify Member notice or cancellation evidence

Confirm that the actual outcome—not just an activity—has been recorded. If the process ended early, use a closed reason rather than deleting the record.

## Copy-and-paste weekly review

- [ ] Review records where an instructor reports an absence
- [ ] Review records where no eligible substitute accepts by the escalation time
- [ ] Review records where a confirmed substitute withdraws or lacks access

- [ ] Check for accepting the first volunteer without checking qualification
- [ ] Check for changing the public schedule before the substitute confirms
- [ ] Check for leaving door or equipment instructions in the absent instructor's inbox
- [ ] Check for forgetting the payroll adjustment after coverage

## Make the checklist measurable

Choose one metric before the next cycle. Good options for this workflow are Coverage fill time, Covered class rate, Late member notice rate. A checklist that never changes a metric or prevents a known failure mode is probably administrative overhead.

## Assign ownership and escalation

Put one role—not a group—next to every item that can remain open. Define a backup owner and an escalation time for work that affects a customer, client, participant, or delivery promise. During review, separate **not started**, **waiting on someone**, and **failed validation**; those states need different actions. If a checklist item repeatedly waits on the same dependency, redesign the intake or handoff instead of adding more reminder boxes.
