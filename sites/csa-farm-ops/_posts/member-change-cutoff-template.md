The most useful CSA skip swap and pickup change tracking template is a small operating record. It should answer what is happening, who owns it, what evidence exists, and when the next decision occurs. This structure works in a spreadsheet, database, or focused application.

## Recommended record fields

| Field | Why it exists | Update point |
|---|---|---|
| Member and subscription | Prevents the record from depending on memory or an inbox search | Capture the member request and effective week |
| Delivery week and pickup site | Prevents the record from depending on memory or an inbox search | Apply plan rules and the relevant cutoff |
| Request type and original message | Prevents the record from depending on memory or an inbox search | Approve the skip, swap, move, or alternative |
| Request time and cutoff | Prevents the record from depending on memory or an inbox search | Update packing, inventory, payment, and route records |
| Eligibility and credit impact | Prevents the record from depending on memory or an inbox search | Confirm the final outcome to the member |
| Approved box or location change | Prevents the record from depending on memory or an inbox search | Capture the member request and effective week |
| Packing and route update evidence | Prevents the record from depending on memory or an inbox search | Apply plan rules and the relevant cutoff |
| Member confirmation or closed alternative | Prevents the record from depending on memory or an inbox search | Approve the skip, swap, move, or alternative |

## Suggested statuses

Use workflow statuses that describe reality: **Capture The Member Request And Effective Week → Apply Plan Rules And The Relevant Cutoff → Approve The Skip Swap Move Or Alternative → Update Packing Inventory Payment And Route Records → Confirm The Final Outcome To The Member**. Add **Waiting** only when you also capture a waiting reason and review date. Add **Closed—Not Completed** when an item legitimately ends without the desired outcome.

## Follow-up rules

- When a request arrives near or after its cutoff, assign a next action and review date.
- When a swap or pickup move lacks inventory or capacity, assign a next action and review date.
- When the member record and frozen packing list disagree, assign a next action and review date.

Avoid reminders with no stop condition. A rule should say when it starts, who receives it, what counts as a response, and when a person should take over.

## Example records

- A member requests a vacation skip after Monday's harvest plan
- Two members swap box sizes for one week
- A pickup-site change updates the profile but not the route sheet

For each example, write the current status, next action, owner, and supporting evidence. This makes the template testable with real work rather than idealized sample data.

## Quality-control rules

- Every open CSA member change needs one owner and a next review time
- Completion requires recorded evidence that every eligible member change is applied before the correct packing and route cutoff or closed with a clear alternative
- Automated reminders stop after verified completion or a documented closed reason
- Keep CSA subscription, payment, packing, and route system as the system of record; only necessary coordination data belongs here

Before adding automation, run the template manually for a week. Remove ambiguous fields and confirm that two different users classify the same situation the same way. Consistency matters more than having a long form.
