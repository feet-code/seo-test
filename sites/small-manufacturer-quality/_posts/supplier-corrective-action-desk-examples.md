Examples make supplier corrective action request tracking easier to design because they reveal where a neat diagram meets messy work. The scenarios below are not claims about a particular company; they are test cases small manufacturers and lean quality teams can run against a template or software trial.

### Scenario 1: A coating defect affects two purchase orders in transit

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Supplier and contact, Part, lot, PO, and quantity, Defect and requirement evidence, then move it through issue the request with defect and lot evidence and confirm supplier containment and exposure. If initial containment is not received by the agreed time, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason. ### Scenario 2: A supplier sends an 8D with no evidence for the stated cause

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Part, lot, PO, and quantity, Defect and requirement evidence, Response level and due dates, then move it through issue the request with defect and lot evidence and confirm supplier containment and exposure. If a proposed cause does not explain the evidence, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason. ### Scenario 3: The first post-action lot repeats the dimensional failure

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Defect and requirement evidence, Response level and due dates, Containment and exposure, then move it through issue the request with defect and lot evidence and confirm supplier containment and exposure. If the defect recurs in a controlled shipment or validation lot, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason.

## Debrief each scenario

After running a scenario, ask:

- Did the record make every open supplier corrective action request needs one owner and a next review time?
- Did the record make completion requires recorded evidence that every material supplier issue requiring corrective action receives accepted containment, cause, action, and effectiveness evidence by agreed deadlines?
- Did the record make automated reminders stop after verified completion or a documented closed reason?
- Did the record make keep approved qms, erp, and controlled-document repository as the system of record; only necessary coordination data belongs here?

Also check whether a new teammate could identify the owner, next action, and finish condition without opening another system.

## Convert scenarios into acceptance tests

Use the normal case, waiting case, and closed-without-completion case in every software demo. Require the vendor—or your own prototype—to show the full workflow rather than isolated feature screens. Export the resulting records and verify that the status history remains understandable.
