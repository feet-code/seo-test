Examples make manufacturing nonconformance closeout easier to design because they reveal where a neat diagram meets messy work. The scenarios below are not claims about a particular company; they are test cases small manufacturers and lean quality teams can run against a template or software trial.

### Scenario 1: A machined lot fails a dimension check after some units shipped

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Part, lot, job, and quantity, Requirement and defect evidence, Detection point and date, then move it through record the requirement and nonconforming evidence and contain affected material and identify scope. If containment is incomplete for the suspected scope, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason. ### Scenario 2: Rework is complete but scrap quantity is not reconciled

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Requirement and defect evidence, Detection point and date, Containment location and scope, then move it through record the requirement and nonconforming evidence and contain affected material and identify scope. If disposition or corrective action passes its due date, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason. ### Scenario 3: The same label defect returns on the next production order

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Detection point and date, Containment location and scope, Disposition and approval, then move it through record the requirement and nonconforming evidence and contain affected material and identify scope. If the same defect appears after effectiveness approval, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason.

## Debrief each scenario

After running a scenario, ask:

- Did the record make every open nonconformance record needs one owner and a next review time?
- Did the record make completion requires recorded evidence that every nonconformance is contained, dispositioned by authority, corrected, and closed only after required effectiveness evidence?
- Did the record make automated reminders stop after verified completion or a documented closed reason?
- Did the record make keep approved qms, erp, and controlled-document repository as the system of record; only necessary coordination data belongs here?

Also check whether a new teammate could identify the owner, next action, and finish condition without opening another system.

## Convert scenarios into acceptance tests

Use the normal case, waiting case, and closed-without-completion case in every software demo. Require the vendor—or your own prototype—to show the full workflow rather than isolated feature screens. Export the resulting records and verify that the status history remains understandable.
