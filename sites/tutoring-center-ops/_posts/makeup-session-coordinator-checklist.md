A checklist for tutoring makeup session scheduling should prevent missing decisions, not merely prove that somebody clicked boxes. The checklist below is designed for independent tutoring centers and multi-tutor education businesses and centers on one result: **every eligible canceled session is rescheduled, credited, expired by policy, or closed with parent acknowledgment**.

## Before the work starts

- Confirm Student and subject
- Confirm Original session and tutor
- Confirm Cancellation party and time
- Confirm Policy and eligibility result

Also name the owner and the expected completion condition. If either is unknown, the work is not ready to enter the active queue.

## While the work is moving

- Update Record the canceled session and policy reason
- Update Determine makeup or credit eligibility
- Update Offer compatible tutor and student times
- Update Confirm the replacement session
- Update Reconcile attendance, credit, and billing

Every update should change a decision. Notes such as “followed up” are weak unless they also include the channel, result, next date, and owner.

## Before marking it complete

- Verify Credit quantity and expiration
- Verify Availability constraints
- Verify Confirmed replacement session
- Verify Attendance and billing reconciliation

Confirm that the actual outcome—not just an activity—has been recorded. If the process ended early, use a closed reason rather than deleting the record.

## Copy-and-paste weekly review

- [ ] Review records where an eligible cancellation has no accepted option
- [ ] Review records where a credit approaches its policy expiration
- [ ] Review records where the confirmed tutor or student becomes unavailable again

- [ ] Check for creating a credit without linking the original session
- [ ] Check for offering a tutor who cannot cover the subject or level
- [ ] Check for leaving an unused credit open past the documented policy
- [ ] Check for charging both the original and replacement session by mistake

## Make the checklist measurable

Choose one metric before the next cycle. Good options for this workflow are Makeup resolution time, Credit aging, Reconciliation error rate. A checklist that never changes a metric or prevents a known failure mode is probably administrative overhead.

## Assign ownership and escalation

Put one role—not a group—next to every item that can remain open. Define a backup owner and an escalation time for work that affects a customer, client, participant, or delivery promise. During review, separate **not started**, **waiting on someone**, and **failed validation**; those states need different actions. If a checklist item repeatedly waits on the same dependency, redesign the intake or handoff instead of adding more reminder boxes.
