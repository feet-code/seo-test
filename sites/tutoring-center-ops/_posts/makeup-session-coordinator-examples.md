Examples make tutoring makeup session scheduling easier to design because they reveal where a neat diagram meets messy work. The scenarios below are not claims about a particular company; they are test cases independent tutoring centers and multi-tutor education businesses can run against a template or software trial.

### Scenario 1: A math tutor is sick and six families need equivalent slots

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Student and subject, Original session and tutor, Cancellation party and time, then move it through record the canceled session and policy reason and determine makeup or credit eligibility. If an eligible cancellation has no accepted option, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason. ### Scenario 2: A parent has two credits but only one was used

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Original session and tutor, Cancellation party and time, Policy and eligibility result, then move it through record the canceled session and policy reason and determine makeup or credit eligibility. If a credit approaches its policy expiration, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason. ### Scenario 3: A rescheduled lesson appears in attendance but not billing

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Cancellation party and time, Policy and eligibility result, Credit quantity and expiration, then move it through record the canceled session and policy reason and determine makeup or credit eligibility. If the confirmed tutor or student becomes unavailable again, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason.

## Debrief each scenario

After running a scenario, ask:

- Did the record make every open makeup session obligation needs one owner and a next review time?
- Did the record make completion requires recorded evidence that every eligible canceled session is rescheduled, credited, expired by policy, or closed with parent acknowledgment?
- Did the record make automated reminders stop after verified completion or a documented closed reason?
- Did the record make keep tutoring schedule and student record system as the system of record; only necessary coordination data belongs here?

Also check whether a new teammate could identify the owner, next action, and finish condition without opening another system.

## Convert scenarios into acceptance tests

Use the normal case, waiting case, and closed-without-completion case in every software demo. Require the vendor—or your own prototype—to show the full workflow rather than isolated feature screens. Export the resulting records and verify that the status history remains understandable.
