Examples make music school makeup lesson credit tracking easier to design because they reveal where a neat diagram meets messy work. The scenarios below are not claims about a particular company; they are test cases independent music schools and multi-teacher lesson studios can run against a template or software trial.

### Scenario 1: A teacher illness creates twelve studio-owned credits

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Student, family, and instrument, Original lesson and teacher, Cancellation party and notice time, then move it through record the missed lesson and cancellation source and apply the current studio policy. If an eligible missed lesson has no resolution, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason. ### Scenario 2: A family asks to use two private credits for a group workshop

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Original lesson and teacher, Cancellation party and notice time, Policy version and eligibility, then move it through record the missed lesson and cancellation source and apply the current studio policy. If a credit approaches expiry, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason. ### Scenario 3: A makeup occurs but the original teacher's pay adjustment is missing

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Cancellation party and notice time, Policy version and eligibility, Credit type, value, and expiry, then move it through record the missed lesson and cancellation source and apply the current studio policy. If a scheduled makeup is canceled or conflicts with teacher eligibility, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason.

## Debrief each scenario

After running a scenario, ask:

- Did the record make every open makeup lesson credit needs one owner and a next review time?
- Did the record make completion requires recorded evidence that every eligible missed lesson becomes one scheduled makeup, valid credit, policy closure, or billing adjustment with a clear expiration?
- Did the record make automated reminders stop after verified completion or a documented closed reason?
- Did the record make keep lesson schedule, attendance, billing, and policy system as the system of record; only necessary coordination data belongs here?

Also check whether a new teammate could identify the owner, next action, and finish condition without opening another system.

## Convert scenarios into acceptance tests

Use the normal case, waiting case, and closed-without-completion case in every software demo. Require the vendor—or your own prototype—to show the full workflow rather than isolated feature screens. Export the resulting records and verify that the status history remains understandable.
