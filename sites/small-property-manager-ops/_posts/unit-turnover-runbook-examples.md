Examples make rental unit turnover checklist and make-ready tracking easier to design because they reveal where a neat diagram meets messy work. The scenarios below are not claims about a particular company; they are test cases independent residential property managers and small property teams can run against a template or software trial.

### Scenario 1: Floor repair pushes cleaning and photography into the same afternoon

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Property and unit, Target ready date, Inspection findings, then move it through complete the move-out inspection and define repair and cleaning scope. If a predecessor task slips past its dependent appointment, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason. ### Scenario 2: An inspection reveals an appliance issue requiring owner approval

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Target ready date, Inspection findings, Task dependency, then move it through complete the move-out inspection and define repair and cleaning scope. If a finding changes the approved repair scope, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason. ### Scenario 3: The unit looks ready in photos but a smoke-alarm check is undocumented

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Inspection findings, Task dependency, Vendor and appointment, then move it through complete the move-out inspection and define repair and cleaning scope. If the target ready date is at risk, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason.

## Debrief each scenario

After running a scenario, ask:

- Did the record make readiness blockers are distinct from cosmetic preferences?
- Did the record make dependent work cannot be marked ready out of sequence?
- Did the record make every task has room-level evidence?
- Did the record make one named person approves the final ready state?

Also check whether a new teammate could identify the owner, next action, and finish condition without opening another system.

## Convert scenarios into acceptance tests

Use the normal case, waiting case, and closed-without-completion case in every software demo. Require the vendor—or your own prototype—to show the full workflow rather than isolated feature screens. Export the resulting records and verify that the status history remains understandable.
