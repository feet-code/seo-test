Software for conference sponsor deliverable tracking should be evaluated against the operating problem, not a generic feature checklist. For independent conference organizers and small trade-show teams, a useful trial must demonstrate this outcome: **every contracted sponsor obligation has an approved input, delivery owner, placement evidence, and accepted outcome**.

## Write requirements from the workflow

The tool must support these steps without hidden spreadsheets: Extract obligations and inputs from the signed package, Assign sponsor contact and internal delivery owner, Collect and approve required assets, Execute and evidence each placement or entitlement, Reconcile delivery and issue the sponsor report. It must also make these fields easy to capture at the moment work happens: Event, sponsor, and package, Contract obligation, Sponsor input and due date, Internal owner and dependency, Asset approval status, Delivery date and placement, Evidence link, Sponsor acceptance, exception, or make-good.

## Use a live demo script

Ask the vendor—or your internal prototype—to complete these tasks:

- Create and resolve this test case: A sponsor logo misses the print deadline
- Create and resolve this test case: A contracted session slot changes rooms
- Create and resolve this test case: Lead-scan reporting needs evidence from the event platform

Then test one waiting case, one reassignment, one closed-without-completion case, and one export. Do not accept a slide deck in place of the workflow.

## Score the trial

| Metric | Simple calculation | Decision it supports |
|---|---|---|
| Obligation completion | obligations delivered / obligations contracted | manage fulfillment risk |
| Sponsor input timeliness | inputs approved by cutoff / inputs due | improve asset chasers |
| Evidence completeness | delivered obligations with proof / obligations marked delivered | support renewal conversations |

Add setup time, recurring administration, export quality, permission clarity, and mobile usability where relevant. Weight the score by frequency: a daily two-minute annoyance matters more than a rare advanced feature.

## Red flags

- Tracking invoice payment but not fulfillment
- Treating a logo upload as placement evidence
- Changing an entitlement without approved make-good
- Building the post-event report from memory

Also be cautious when the product requires broad process migration before it can solve the narrow problem, or when basic history/export controls are unavailable.

## Make the decision with real records

Run a small trial using current work, not sanitized sample data. Compare the realistic alternatives below and record why the winning approach fits now:

| Approach | Best when | Main limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Email threads, asset folders, sponsorship sheets, and agenda documents | One owner handles low volume and can see every open item | Status and follow-up history depend on memory and inbox searches |
| Event-management software or a shared stakeholder-delivery board | The team already maintains it and exceptions are simple | Purpose-built reminders, evidence, and stop conditions require manual setup |
| A focused workflow tool | The same coordination failure repeats across many live records | It must integrate with the system of record and justify another workflow |
