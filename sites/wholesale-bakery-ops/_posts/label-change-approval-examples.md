Examples make wholesale bakery allergen and label change approval easier to design because they reveal where a neat diagram meets messy work. The scenarios below are not claims about a particular company; they are test cases small wholesale and direct-store-delivery bakeries can run against a template or software trial.

### Scenario 1: A supplier changes an ingredient subcomponent

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Product SKU and customer variant, Change source reason and requested date, Old and new ingredient or recipe version, then move it through open change from recipe supplier or requirement and assess ingredient allergen claim and package impact. If an ingredient supplier recipe or claim changes, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason. ### Scenario 2: A grocery account revises its address block

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Change source reason and requested date, Old and new ingredient or recipe version, Allergen nutrition claim and net-content impact, then move it through open change from recipe supplier or requirement and assess ingredient allergen claim and package impact. If a customer requests a private-label revision, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason. ### Scenario 3: A new bag size changes net-weight presentation

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Old and new ingredient or recipe version, Allergen nutrition claim and net-content impact, Artwork file revision and printer, then move it through open change from recipe supplier or requirement and assess ingredient allergen claim and package impact. If the first production check differs from approved artwork, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason.

## Debrief each scenario

After running a scenario, ask:

- Did the record make every open label version change needs one owner and a next review time?
- Did the record make completion requires recorded evidence that every label change is reviewed by the responsible people, tied to effective product and lot boundaries, and verified at first production use?
- Did the record make automated reminders stop after verified completion or a documented closed reason?
- Did the record make keep the bakery erp, recipe, allergen, label, production, lot, order, route, and billing platform as the system of record; only necessary coordination data belongs here?

Also check whether a new teammate could identify the owner, next action, and finish condition without opening another system.

## Convert scenarios into acceptance tests

Use the normal case, waiting case, and closed-without-completion case in every software demo. Require the vendor—or your own prototype—to show the full workflow rather than isolated feature screens. Export the resulting records and verify that the status history remains understandable.
