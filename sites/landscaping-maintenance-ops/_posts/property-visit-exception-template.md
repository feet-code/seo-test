The most useful landscape maintenance visit exception tracking template is a small operating record. It should answer what is happening, who owns it, what evidence exists, and when the next decision occurs. This structure works in a spreadsheet, database, or focused application.

## Recommended record fields

| Field | Why it exists | Update point |
|---|---|---|
| Customer property and contract | Prevents the record from depending on memory or an inbox search | Record the exception at the property |
| Visit date crew and route | Prevents the record from depending on memory or an inbox search | Separate completed from blocked scope |

## Suggested statuses

Use workflow statuses that describe reality: **Record The Exception At The Property → Separate Completed From Blocked Scope → Apply Contract And Operations Rules → Notify The Customer And Schedule Recovery → Verify Recovered Work Or Approved Closure**. Add **Waiting** only when you also capture a waiting reason and review date. Add **Closed—Not Completed** when an item legitimately ends without the desired outcome.

## Follow-up rules

- When a crew cannot complete planned scope, assign a next action and review date.
- When an exception changes billing or customer expectation, assign a next action and review date.
- When the promised recovery window becomes at risk, assign a next action and review date.

Avoid reminders with no stop condition. A rule should say when it starts, who receives it, what counts as a response, and when a person should take over.

## Example records

- Parked cars block an edging area
- Rain stops mowing after bed service is complete
- An event prevents access to half a commercial property

For each example, write the current status, next action, owner, and supporting evidence. This makes the template testable with real work rather than idealized sample data.

## Quality-control rules

- Every open visit exception needs one owner and a next review time
- Completion requires recorded evidence that every incomplete landscape visit has quantified skipped work, evidence, contract treatment, customer communication, and a recovery decision
- Automated reminders stop after verified completion or a documented closed reason
- Keep the landscape CRM, contract, estimate, route, crew, job-cost, and billing platform as the system of record; only necessary coordination data belongs here

Before adding automation, run the template manually for a week. Remove ambiguous fields and confirm that two different users classify the same situation the same way. Consistency matters more than having a long form.
