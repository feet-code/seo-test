Examples make landscape enhancement proposal approval tracking easier to design because they reveal where a neat diagram meets messy work. The scenarios below are not claims about a particular company; they are test cases small commercial landscaping and recurring property-maintenance companies can run against a template or software trial.

### Scenario 1: A crew documents a failed shrub row

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Customer property and contract, Request source and desired outcome, Location photos and measurements, then move it through capture the opportunity from field or customer and qualify site condition and desired result. If a crew records work outside recurring scope, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason. ### Scenario 2: A manager requests fresh mulch before an event

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Request source and desired outcome, Location photos and measurements, Existing condition and constraints, then move it through capture the opportunity from field or customer and qualify site condition and desired result. If the customer requests a property improvement, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason. ### Scenario 3: Drainage evidence changes the proposed grading scope

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Location photos and measurements, Existing condition and constraints, Scope option materials and labor, then move it through capture the opportunity from field or customer and qualify site condition and desired result. If conditions or price assumptions change before approval, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason.

## Debrief each scenario

After running a scenario, ask:

- Did the record make every open enhancement opportunity needs one owner and a next review time?
- Did the record make completion requires recorded evidence that every qualified enhancement request reaches a priced customer decision with field context, scope assumptions, and a scheduled or closed next action?
- Did the record make automated reminders stop after verified completion or a documented closed reason?
- Did the record make keep the landscape crm, contract, estimate, route, crew, job-cost, and billing platform as the system of record; only necessary coordination data belongs here?

Also check whether a new teammate could identify the owner, next action, and finish condition without opening another system.

## Convert scenarios into acceptance tests

Use the normal case, waiting case, and closed-without-completion case in every software demo. Require the vendor—or your own prototype—to show the full workflow rather than isolated feature screens. Export the resulting records and verify that the status history remains understandable.
