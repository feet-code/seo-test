A checklist for makerspace equipment training authorization tracking should prevent missing decisions, not merely prove that somebody clicked boxes. The checklist below is designed for community makerspaces, fabrication labs, and shared technical workshops and centers on one result: **every equipment access grant is tied to current membership, documented prerequisites, named trainer approval, policy version, and matching access-control state**.

## Before the work starts

- Confirm Member membership and status
- Confirm Equipment and authorization level
- Confirm Policy waiver and orientation version
- Confirm Training date curriculum and trainer

Also name the owner and the expected completion condition. If either is unknown, the work is not ready to enter the active queue.

## While the work is moving

- Update Create prerequisites from equipment and policy
- Update Collect training attendance and practical check
- Update Record trainer decision limits and expiry
- Update Publish authorization to booking and access systems
- Update Review suspension renewal and exception events

Every update should change a decision. Notes such as “followed up” are weak unless they also include the channel, result, next date, and owner.

## Before marking it complete

- Verify Practical check evidence and decision
- Verify Restrictions expiry and renewal rule
- Verify Booking and access-control publication
- Verify Suspension exception and review history

Confirm that the actual outcome—not just an activity—has been recorded. If the process ended early, use a closed reason rather than deleting the record.

## Copy-and-paste weekly review

- [ ] Review records where a member requests machine access
- [ ] Review records where training membership policy or suspension status changes
- [ ] Review records where booking or door control disagrees with authorization

- [ ] Check for granting access from attendance alone
- [ ] Check for letting a peer approve without trainer authority
- [ ] Check for keeping access active after membership or authorization expiry
- [ ] Check for editing the original training record after an incident

## Make the checklist measurable

Choose one metric before the next cycle. Good options for this workflow are Authorization publication time, Access-state accuracy, Expired-use attempts. A checklist that never changes a metric or prevents a known failure mode is probably administrative overhead.

## Assign ownership and escalation

Put one role—not a group—next to every item that can remain open. Define a backup owner and an escalation time for work that affects a customer, client, participant, or delivery promise. During review, separate **not started**, **waiting on someone**, and **failed validation**; those states need different actions. If a checklist item repeatedly waits on the same dependency, redesign the intake or handoff instead of adding more reminder boxes.
