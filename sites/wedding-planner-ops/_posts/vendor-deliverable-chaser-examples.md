Examples make wedding vendor deliverable tracking easier to design because they reveal where a neat diagram meets messy work. The scenarios below are not claims about a particular company; they are test cases independent wedding planners and boutique planning teams can run against a template or software trial.

### Scenario 1: The caterer owes a final menu and allergen matrix

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Wedding and vendor, Contract requirement, Deliverable description, then move it through extract the deliverable and deadline from the contract and assign the vendor contact and internal reviewer. If a deliverable is not received by its reminder threshold, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason. ### Scenario 2: The rental company sends a floor plan from an older guest count

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Contract requirement, Deliverable description, Due date and dependency date, then move it through extract the deliverable and deadline from the contract and assign the vendor contact and internal reviewer. If a submitted file conflicts with the contract or current plan, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason. ### Scenario 3: A venue needs an updated insurance certificate before access

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Deliverable description, Due date and dependency date, Vendor contact and planner owner, then move it through extract the deliverable and deadline from the contract and assign the vendor contact and internal reviewer. If a wedding decision changes a previously approved vendor requirement, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason.

## Debrief each scenario

After running a scenario, ask:

- Did the record make every open vendor deliverable needs one owner and a next review time?
- Did the record make completion requires recorded evidence that every contracted vendor deliverable is received, reviewed, and reflected in the current wedding plan before its dependency date?
- Did the record make automated reminders stop after verified completion or a documented closed reason?
- Did the record make keep approved wedding plan, contract, and project workspace as the system of record; only necessary coordination data belongs here?

Also check whether a new teammate could identify the owner, next action, and finish condition without opening another system.

## Convert scenarios into acceptance tests

Use the normal case, waiting case, and closed-without-completion case in every software demo. Require the vendor—or your own prototype—to show the full workflow rather than isolated feature screens. Export the resulting records and verify that the status history remains understandable.
