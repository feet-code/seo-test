A checklist for funeral arrangement document readiness tracking should prevent missing decisions, not merely prove that somebody clicked boxes. The checklist below is designed for independent funeral homes and small death-care service teams and centers on one result: **every case has human-reviewed required decisions, documents, authorizations, and exceptions visible against the actual service timeline**.

## Before the work starts

- Confirm Case family and responsible arranger
- Confirm Service dates locations and case type
- Confirm Required information and source
- Confirm Decision merchandise and personalization status

Also name the owner and the expected completion condition. If either is unknown, the work is not ready to enter the active queue.

## While the work is moving

- Update Create requirements from case type and selected services
- Update Collect family information decisions and signatures
- Update Review completeness without making family choices
- Update Resolve permit payment and third-party exceptions
- Update Release each service milestone through an authorized director

Every update should change a decision. Notes such as “followed up” are weak unless they also include the channel, result, next date, and owner.

## Before marking it complete

- Verify Authorization signature and reviewer
- Verify Permit filing and external confirmation
- Verify Contract payment and exception
- Verify Director milestone release and audit time

Confirm that the actual outcome—not just an activity—has been recorded. If the process ended early, use a closed reason rather than deleting the record.

## Copy-and-paste weekly review

- [ ] Review records where a case or selected service creates a requirement
- [ ] Review records where family information authorization or third-party confirmation is missing
- [ ] Review records where the service timeline changes the applicable cutoff

- [ ] Check for treating a form upload as reviewed
- [ ] Check for automating a sensitive family decision
- [ ] Check for using one requirement list for every jurisdiction or case type
- [ ] Check for closing readiness before an authorized director releases the milestone

## Make the checklist measurable

Choose one metric before the next cycle. Good options for this workflow are Ready-by-cutoff rate, First-review completeness, Unowned exception rate. A checklist that never changes a metric or prevents a known failure mode is probably administrative overhead.

## Assign ownership and escalation

Put one role—not a group—next to every item that can remain open. Define a backup owner and an escalation time for work that affects a customer, client, participant, or delivery promise. During review, separate **not started**, **waiting on someone**, and **failed validation**; those states need different actions. If a checklist item repeatedly waits on the same dependency, redesign the intake or handoff instead of adding more reminder boxes.
