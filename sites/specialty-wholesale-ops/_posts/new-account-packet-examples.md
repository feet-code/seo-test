Examples make wholesale customer onboarding and new account setup checklists easier to design because they reveal where a neat diagram meets messy work. The scenarios below are not claims about a particular company; they are test cases small specialty wholesalers and B2B distributors can run against a template or software trial.

### Scenario 1: A prepaid account is incorrectly routed through a credit-terms approval

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Legal business name, Billing and ship-to contacts, Ordering contact, then move it through select the account path and request customer information and documents. If a required document is missing or rejected, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason. ### Scenario 2: A resale document is uploaded with a mismatched legal name

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Billing and ship-to contacts, Ordering contact, Tax or resale document status, then move it through select the account path and request customer information and documents. If requested terms require internal review, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason. ### Scenario 3: The account is approved but the buyer never receives ordering instructions

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Ordering contact, Tax or resale document status, Payment terms decision, then move it through select the account path and request customer information and documents. If the first-order path is not configured by the target ready date, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason.

## Debrief each scenario

After running a scenario, ask:

- Did the record make the packet branches by account type?
- Did the record make sensitive documents use appropriate secure handling?
- Did the record make ready-to-order has an explicit checklist?
- Did the record make the customer receives next-step instructions and an owner?

Also check whether a new teammate could identify the owner, next action, and finish condition without opening another system.

## Convert scenarios into acceptance tests

Use the normal case, waiting case, and closed-without-completion case in every software demo. Require the vendor—or your own prototype—to show the full workflow rather than isolated feature screens. Export the resulting records and verify that the status history remains understandable.
