Examples make wholesale customer reorder reminders and account follow-up easier to design because they reveal where a neat diagram meets messy work. The scenarios below are not claims about a particular company; they are test cases small specialty wholesalers and B2B distributors can run against a template or software trial.

### Scenario 1: A restaurant account usually reorders packaging monthly but is closed for renovation

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Account, Item family, Prior order date, then move it through establish the account-item cadence and create an explainable review date. If the review date arrives with no open order, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason. ### Scenario 2: A retailer may need seasonal stock earlier than last year

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Item family, Prior order date, Typical interval, then move it through establish the account-item cadence and create an explainable review date. If core items are backordered or substituted, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason. ### Scenario 3: A buyer changed roles and the prior rep contact is no longer valid

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Prior order date, Typical interval, Season or event, then move it through establish the account-item cadence and create an explainable review date. If the account reports a changed season, project, or purchasing policy, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason.

## Debrief each scenario

After running a scenario, ask:

- Did the record make signals explain why they appeared?
- Did the record make a person reviews context before outreach?
- Did the record make no model invents customer inventory?
- Did the record make outcome data improves future review timing?

Also check whether a new teammate could identify the owner, next action, and finish condition without opening another system.

## Convert scenarios into acceptance tests

Use the normal case, waiting case, and closed-without-completion case in every software demo. Require the vendor—or your own prototype—to show the full workflow rather than isolated feature screens. Export the resulting records and verify that the status history remains understandable.
