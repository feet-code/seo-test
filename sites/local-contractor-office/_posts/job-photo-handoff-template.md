The most useful contractor job photo documentation and field office handoff template is a small operating record. It should answer what is happening, who owns it, what evidence exists, and when the next decision occurs. This structure works in a spreadsheet, database, or focused application.

## Recommended record fields

## Suggested statuses

Use workflow statuses that describe reality: **Define The Required Photo Set → Capture Photos At The Correct Job Stage → Label Context And Exceptions → Submit The Field Closeout → Review And Release The Office Workflow**. Add **Waiting** only when you also capture a waiting reason and review date. Add **Closed—Not Completed** when an item legitimately ends without the desired outcome.

## Follow-up rules

- When a required stage photo is missing, assign a next action and review date.
- When the technician identifies changed scope or concealed conditions, assign a next action and review date.
- When an image may contain customer-sensitive information, assign a next action and review date.

Avoid reminders with no stop condition. A rule should say when it starts, who receives it, what counts as a response, and when a person should take over.

## Example records

- A technician photographs new equipment but not the removed unit or nameplate
- A concealed pipe condition changes the quoted scope
- A completed repair photo includes unrelated personal material in the background

For each example, write the current status, next action, owner, and supporting evidence. This makes the template testable with real work rather than idealized sample data.

## Quality-control rules

- Every image is linked to a job and purpose
- Required stages are defined before the visit
- Sensitive images have access and sharing rules
- The office can reject an incomplete handoff before billing

Before adding automation, run the template manually for a week. Remove ambiguous fields and confirm that two different users classify the same situation the same way. Consistency matters more than having a long form.
