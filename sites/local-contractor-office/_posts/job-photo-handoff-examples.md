Examples make contractor job photo documentation and field office handoff easier to design because they reveal where a neat diagram meets messy work. The scenarios below are not claims about a particular company; they are test cases owner-operated HVAC, plumbing, electrical, and repair contractors can run against a template or software trial.

### Scenario 1: A technician photographs new equipment but not the removed unit or nameplate

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Customer and job number, Technician, Photo stage, then move it through define the required photo set and capture photos at the correct job stage. If a required stage photo is missing, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason. ### Scenario 2: A concealed pipe condition changes the quoted scope

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Technician, Photo stage, Equipment or area, then move it through define the required photo set and capture photos at the correct job stage. If the technician identifies changed scope or concealed conditions, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason. ### Scenario 3: A completed repair photo includes unrelated personal material in the background

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Photo stage, Equipment or area, Caption, then move it through define the required photo set and capture photos at the correct job stage. If an image may contain customer-sensitive information, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason.

## Debrief each scenario

After running a scenario, ask:

- Did the record make every image is linked to a job and purpose?
- Did the record make required stages are defined before the visit?
- Did the record make sensitive images have access and sharing rules?
- Did the record make the office can reject an incomplete handoff before billing?

Also check whether a new teammate could identify the owner, next action, and finish condition without opening another system.

## Convert scenarios into acceptance tests

Use the normal case, waiting case, and closed-without-completion case in every software demo. Require the vendor—or your own prototype—to show the full workflow rather than isolated feature screens. Export the resulting records and verify that the status history remains understandable.
