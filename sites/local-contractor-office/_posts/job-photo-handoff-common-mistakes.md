Job photos remain on technicians' phones or in group chat without labels, making office review, invoicing, warranty response, and customer updates harder. The recurring failures are usually process-design problems rather than motivation problems. For owner-operated HVAC, plumbing, electrical, and repair contractors, these are the mistakes worth finding before buying or building software.


### 1. Uploading photos with no job or area label

This usually survives because the workflow records activity but not the decision that activity was meant to produce. Add **Technician** at the point of work and enforce this guardrail: Required stages are defined before the visit When the exception occurs, keep it visible instead of repairing it privately in email.

### 2. Taking only completion photos when before evidence matters

This usually survives because the workflow records activity but not the decision that activity was meant to produce. Add **Photo stage** at the point of work and enforce this guardrail: Sensitive images have access and sharing rules When the exception occurs, keep it visible instead of repairing it privately in email.

### 3. Mixing private/internal and customer-facing images

This usually survives because the workflow records activity but not the decision that activity was meant to produce. Add **Equipment or area** at the point of work and enforce this guardrail: The office can reject an incomplete handoff before billing When the exception occurs, keep it visible instead of repairing it privately in email.

### 4. Closing the job before the office confirms the required set

This usually survives because the workflow records activity but not the decision that activity was meant to produce. Add **Caption** at the point of work and enforce this guardrail: Every image is linked to a job and purpose When the exception occurs, keep it visible instead of repairing it privately in email.

## Audit five recent records

Pick five completed or abandoned examples and ask:

- Can we reconstruct customer and job number without asking the original owner?
- Can we reconstruct technician without asking the original owner?
- Can we reconstruct photo stage without asking the original owner?
- Can we reconstruct equipment or area without asking the original owner?
- Can we reconstruct caption without asking the original owner?

If the answer is no, improve the capture point rather than adding a later reporting step. Reports cannot recover decisions that were never recorded.

## Use mistakes as software requirements

Turn every frequent failure into a testable requirement. “Better visibility” is vague; “show every record with no owner or next date” can be tested. “More automation” is vague; “stop reminders after the completion condition is recorded” can be tested.
