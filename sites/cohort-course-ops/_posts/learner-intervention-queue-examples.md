Examples make cohort course learner engagement and intervention tracking easier to design because they reveal where a neat diagram meets messy work. The scenarios below are not claims about a particular company; they are test cases independent cohort-course creators and small training businesses can run against a template or software trial.

### Scenario 1: A learner misses one live session but submitted the assignment early

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Cohort and learner, Signal and source, Signal date, then move it through define transparent engagement signals and review the learner context. If a defined combination of attendance, assignment, or communication signals appears, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason. ### Scenario 2: A participant stops posting after reporting a work deadline

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Signal and source, Signal date, Context known, then move it through define transparent engagement signals and review the learner context. If the learner requests help or a schedule change, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason. ### Scenario 3: A learner asks to defer and needs a clear transition plan

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Signal date, Context known, Outreach owner, then move it through define transparent engagement signals and review the learner context. If outreach receives no response by the agreed review date, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason.

## Debrief each scenario

After running a scenario, ask:

- Did the record make signals prompt review; they do not label the learner?
- Did the record make outreach is supportive and specific?
- Did the record make only necessary information is recorded?
- Did the record make a sent message is not the same as a resolved intervention?

Also check whether a new teammate could identify the owner, next action, and finish condition without opening another system.

## Convert scenarios into acceptance tests

Use the normal case, waiting case, and closed-without-completion case in every software demo. Require the vendor—or your own prototype—to show the full workflow rather than isolated feature screens. Export the resulting records and verify that the status history remains understandable.
