The most useful cohort course learner engagement and intervention tracking template is a small operating record. It should answer what is happening, who owns it, what evidence exists, and when the next decision occurs. This structure works in a spreadsheet, database, or focused application.

## Suggested statuses

Use workflow statuses that describe reality: **Define Transparent Engagement Signals → Review The Learner Context → Assign Personal Outreach → Agree On A Support Next Step → Record The Outcome And Next Review**. Add **Waiting** only when you also capture a waiting reason and review date. Add **Closed—Not Completed** when an item legitimately ends without the desired outcome.

## Follow-up rules

- When a defined combination of attendance, assignment, or communication signals appears, assign a next action and review date.
- When the learner requests help or a schedule change, assign a next action and review date.
- When outreach receives no response by the agreed review date, assign a next action and review date.

Avoid reminders with no stop condition. A rule should say when it starts, who receives it, what counts as a response, and when a person should take over.

## Example records

- A learner misses one live session but submitted the assignment early
- A participant stops posting after reporting a work deadline
- A learner asks to defer and needs a clear transition plan

For each example, write the current status, next action, owner, and supporting evidence. This makes the template testable with real work rather than idealized sample data.

## Quality-control rules

- Signals prompt review; they do not label the learner
- Outreach is supportive and specific
- Only necessary information is recorded
- A sent message is not the same as a resolved intervention

Before adding automation, run the template manually for a week. Remove ambiguous fields and confirm that two different users classify the same situation the same way. Consistency matters more than having a long form.
