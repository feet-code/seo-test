Attendance, assignment, and community signals live in separate tools, so struggling learners are noticed late or receive generic reminders. The recurring failures are usually process-design problems rather than motivation problems. For independent cohort-course creators and small training businesses, these are the mistakes worth finding before buying or building software.


### 1. Treating one missed event as proof of disengagement

This usually survives because the workflow records activity but not the decision that activity was meant to produce. Add **Signal and source** at the point of work and enforce this guardrail: Outreach is supportive and specific When the exception occurs, keep it visible instead of repairing it privately in email.

### 2. Sending automated pressure without checking context

This usually survives because the workflow records activity but not the decision that activity was meant to produce. Add **Signal date** at the point of work and enforce this guardrail: Only necessary information is recorded When the exception occurs, keep it visible instead of repairing it privately in email.

### 3. Collecting sensitive learner information that is not needed

This usually survives because the workflow records activity but not the decision that activity was meant to produce. Add **Context known** at the point of work and enforce this guardrail: A sent message is not the same as a resolved intervention When the exception occurs, keep it visible instead of repairing it privately in email.

### 4. Closing the record because a message was sent

This usually survives because the workflow records activity but not the decision that activity was meant to produce. Add **Outreach owner** at the point of work and enforce this guardrail: Signals prompt review; they do not label the learner When the exception occurs, keep it visible instead of repairing it privately in email.

## Audit five recent records

Pick five completed or abandoned examples and ask:

- Can we reconstruct cohort and learner without asking the original owner?
- Can we reconstruct signal and source without asking the original owner?
- Can we reconstruct signal date without asking the original owner?
- Can we reconstruct context known without asking the original owner?
- Can we reconstruct outreach owner without asking the original owner?

If the answer is no, improve the capture point rather than adding a later reporting step. Reports cannot recover decisions that were never recorded.

## Use mistakes as software requirements

Turn every frequent failure into a testable requirement. “Better visibility” is vague; “show every record with no owner or next date” can be tested. “More automation” is vague; “stop reminders after the completion condition is recorded” can be tested.
