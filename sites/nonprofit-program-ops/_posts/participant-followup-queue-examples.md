Examples make nonprofit participant follow-up and referral tracking easier to design because they reveal where a neat diagram meets messy work. The scenarios below are not claims about a particular company; they are test cases small nonprofit direct-service and program teams can run against a template or software trial.

### Scenario 1: A participant requests a resource list by text after a workshop

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Program and participant ID, Follow-up purpose, Consent boundary, then move it through record the follow-up commitment and confirm consent and preferred contact. If a promised follow-up date arrives, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason. ### Scenario 2: A referral is sent but the partner cannot reach the participant

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Follow-up purpose, Consent boundary, Preferred channel, then move it through record the follow-up commitment and confirm consent and preferred contact. If a referral has no confirmation by its review date, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason. ### Scenario 3: A staff member leaves with open check-ins assigned

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Consent boundary, Preferred channel, Owner, then move it through record the follow-up commitment and confirm consent and preferred contact. If staff ownership or participant contact preference changes, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason.

## Debrief each scenario

After running a scenario, ask:

- Did the record make collect only information needed for the action?
- Did the record make consent and contact preferences control outreach?
- Did the record make referral sent and referral connected are separate outcomes?
- Did the record make supervisors can reassign open commitments without exposing unnecessary detail?

Also check whether a new teammate could identify the owner, next action, and finish condition without opening another system.

## Convert scenarios into acceptance tests

Use the normal case, waiting case, and closed-without-completion case in every software demo. Require the vendor—or your own prototype—to show the full workflow rather than isolated feature screens. Export the resulting records and verify that the status history remains understandable.
