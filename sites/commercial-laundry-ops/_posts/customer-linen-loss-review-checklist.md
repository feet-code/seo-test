A checklist for commercial laundry linen loss and replacement tracking should prevent missing decisions, not merely prove that somebody clicked boxes. The checklist below is designed for small commercial laundries and linen or uniform rental services and centers on one result: **every material textile-loss difference is reconstructed, reviewed with the customer, and resolved to count correction, replacement, charge, or process action**.

## Before the work starts

- Confirm Customer, location, and review period
- Confirm Textile item and ownership model
- Confirm Opening circulating balance
- Confirm Delivered and returned quantity

Also name the owner and the expected completion condition. If either is unknown, the work is not ready to enter the active queue.

## While the work is moving

- Update Open the review from a count or replacement threshold
- Update Reconstruct deliveries, returns, discards, and adjustments
- Update Validate item identity and unit conventions
- Update Review responsibility and proposed resolution
- Update Post the approved outcome and monitor the next cycle

Every update should change a decision. Notes such as “followed up” are weak unless they also include the channel, result, next date, and owner.

## Before marking it complete

- Verify Documented discard, damage, and adjustment
- Verify Count method and evidence
- Verify Variance, reviewer, and proposed cause
- Verify Approved charge, replacement, correction, or action

Confirm that the actual outcome—not just an activity—has been recorded. If the process ended early, use a closed reason rather than deleting the record.

## Copy-and-paste weekly review

- [ ] Review records where circulating balance exceeds the review threshold
- [ ] Review records where route or plant evidence changes the proposed variance
- [ ] Review records where customer disputes a charge or the next count repeats the difference

- [ ] Check for comparing pieces with bundles or weight
- [ ] Check for calling every variance customer loss
- [ ] Check for charging replacement before reconciling plant discards
- [ ] Check for resetting the balance without preserving the investigation

## Make the checklist measurable

Choose one metric before the next cycle. Good options for this workflow are Explained variance rate, Loss review cycle, Repeat variance. A checklist that never changes a metric or prevents a known failure mode is probably administrative overhead.

## Assign ownership and escalation

Put one role—not a group—next to every item that can remain open. Define a backup owner and an escalation time for work that affects a customer, client, participant, or delivery promise. During review, separate **not started**, **waiting on someone**, and **failed validation**; those states need different actions. If a checklist item repeatedly waits on the same dependency, redesign the intake or handoff instead of adding more reminder boxes.
