Examples make janitorial supply inventory and location replenishment tracking easier to design because they reveal where a neat diagram meets messy work. The scenarios below are not claims about a particular company; they are test cases owner-operated commercial cleaning and janitorial companies can run against a template or software trial.

### Scenario 1: A site runs out of liners after an event-heavy weekend

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Client location, Storage area, Item and unit, then move it through define the item and par level and count usable stock. If usable stock falls below the reorder point, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason. ### Scenario 2: Two crews count the same chemical in different units

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Storage area, Item and unit, Approved product, then move it through define the item and par level and count usable stock. If usage changes sharply from the prior count, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason. ### Scenario 3: A substitute paper product does not fit the installed dispenser

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Item and unit, Approved product, Par level, then move it through define the item and par level and count usable stock. If an approved item is unavailable or substituted, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason.

## Debrief each scenario

After running a scenario, ask:

- Did the record make every quantity has a unit?
- Did the record make only usable and accessible stock counts?
- Did the record make substitutions require compatibility confirmation?
- Did the record make delivery closes at the client storage location?

Also check whether a new teammate could identify the owner, next action, and finish condition without opening another system.

## Convert scenarios into acceptance tests

Use the normal case, waiting case, and closed-without-completion case in every software demo. Require the vendor—or your own prototype—to show the full workflow rather than isolated feature screens. Export the resulting records and verify that the status history remains understandable.
