Inspection scores and photos are collected, but individual findings are not consistently assigned and verified before the next service cycle. The recurring failures are usually process-design problems rather than motivation problems. For owner-operated commercial cleaning and janitorial companies, these are the mistakes worth finding before buying or building software.


### 1. Saving only an overall inspection score

This usually survives because the workflow records activity but not the decision that activity was meant to produce. Add **Area or fixture** at the point of work and enforce this guardrail: Severity has a defined reason When the exception occurs, keep it visible instead of repairing it privately in email.

### 2. Assigning the whole inspection instead of each finding

This usually survives because the workflow records activity but not the decision that activity was meant to produce. Add **Finding** at the point of work and enforce this guardrail: The person completing work is not the only verifier for serious issues When the exception occurs, keep it visible instead of repairing it privately in email.

### 3. Accepting a completion photo with no location context

This usually survives because the workflow records activity but not the decision that activity was meant to produce. Add **Inspection photo** at the point of work and enforce this guardrail: Repeat findings trigger a workflow change, not another identical reminder When the exception occurs, keep it visible instead of repairing it privately in email.

### 4. Closing repeat findings without changing the instruction or training

This usually survives because the workflow records activity but not the decision that activity was meant to produce. Add **Severity** at the point of work and enforce this guardrail: Findings are assigned individually When the exception occurs, keep it visible instead of repairing it privately in email.

## Audit five recent records

Pick five completed or abandoned examples and ask:

- Can we reconstruct client and location without asking the original owner?
- Can we reconstruct area or fixture without asking the original owner?
- Can we reconstruct finding without asking the original owner?
- Can we reconstruct inspection photo without asking the original owner?
- Can we reconstruct severity without asking the original owner?

If the answer is no, improve the capture point rather than adding a later reporting step. Reports cannot recover decisions that were never recorded.

## Use mistakes as software requirements

Turn every frequent failure into a testable requirement. “Better visibility” is vague; “show every record with no owner or next date” can be tested. “More automation” is vague; “stop reminders after the completion condition is recorded” can be tested.
