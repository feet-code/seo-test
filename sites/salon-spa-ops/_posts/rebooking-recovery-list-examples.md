Examples make salon and spa rebooking follow-up easier to design because they reveal where a neat diagram meets messy work. The scenarios below are not claims about a particular company; they are test cases independent salons, spas, and small wellness studios can run against a template or software trial.

### Scenario 1: A color client should return in six weeks but leaves undecided

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Client and preferred channel, Completed service and provider, Recommended return window, then move it through capture the recommended return window at checkout and separate booked clients from open opportunities. If a client leaves without booking inside the recommended window, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason. ### Scenario 2: A massage client cancels and asks to revisit next month

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Completed service and provider, Recommended return window, Rebooking eligibility, then move it through capture the recommended return window at checkout and separate booked clients from open opportunities. If an appointment is canceled without a replacement, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason. ### Scenario 3: A stylist goes on leave and open rebooking opportunities need alternatives

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Recommended return window, Rebooking eligibility, Next outreach date, then move it through capture the recommended return window at checkout and separate booked clients from open opportunities. If a preferred provider or time is unavailable, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason.

## Debrief each scenario

After running a scenario, ask:

- Did the record make every open rebooking opportunity needs one owner and a next review time?
- Did the record make completion requires recorded evidence that every eligible client receives a timely, contextual rebooking option or a documented no-contact reason?
- Did the record make automated reminders stop after verified completion or a documented closed reason?
- Did the record make keep booking and point-of-sale platform as the system of record; only necessary coordination data belongs here?

Also check whether a new teammate could identify the owner, next action, and finish condition without opening another system.

## Convert scenarios into acceptance tests

Use the normal case, waiting case, and closed-without-completion case in every software demo. Require the vendor—or your own prototype—to show the full workflow rather than isolated feature screens. Export the resulting records and verify that the status history remains understandable.
