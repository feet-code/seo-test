The most useful influencer product sample tracking template is a small operating record. It should answer what is happening, who owns it, what evidence exists, and when the next decision occurs. This structure works in a spreadsheet, database, or focused application.

## Recommended record fields

| Field | Why it exists | Update point |
|---|---|---|
| Creator identity and channel | Prevents the record from depending on memory or an inbox search | Qualify the creator and record the proposed exchange |
| Campaign and product SKU | Prevents the record from depending on memory or an inbox search | Confirm address, consent, and terms |
| Relationship type and terms | Prevents the record from depending on memory or an inbox search | Reserve and ship the sample |
| Consent and shipping address | Prevents the record from depending on memory or an inbox search | Track delivery and contextual follow-up |
| Shipment and delivery evidence | Prevents the record from depending on memory or an inbox search | Record content, rights, return, or closed outcome |
| Deliverable and expected window | Prevents the record from depending on memory or an inbox search | Qualify the creator and record the proposed exchange |
| Follow-up owner and status | Prevents the record from depending on memory or an inbox search | Confirm address, consent, and terms |
| Content link, usage rights, or closed reason | Prevents the record from depending on memory or an inbox search | Reserve and ship the sample |

## Suggested statuses

Use workflow statuses that describe reality: **Qualify The Creator And Record The Proposed Exchange → Confirm Address Consent And Terms → Reserve And Ship The Sample → Track Delivery And Contextual Follow Up → Record Content Rights Return Or Closed Outcome**. Add **Waiting** only when you also capture a waiting reason and review date. Add **Closed—Not Completed** when an item legitimately ends without the desired outcome.

## Follow-up rules

- When a sample is unshipped near the campaign window, assign a next action and review date.
- When delivery fails or the creator changes address, assign a next action and review date.
- When a contracted deliverable is due or content rights are unclear, assign a next action and review date.

Avoid reminders with no stop condition. A rule should say when it starts, who receives it, what counts as a response, and when a person should take over.

## Example records

- A creator accepts a no-obligation product gift
- A paid partner owes two videos after delivery
- A sample is returned because an address from a DM was incomplete

For each example, write the current status, next action, owner, and supporting evidence. This makes the template testable with real work rather than idealized sample data.

## Quality-control rules

- Every open creator sample commitment needs one owner and a next review time
- Completion requires recorded evidence that every creator sample has documented consent and terms, confirmed delivery, and a closed deliverable or no-obligation outcome
- Automated reminders stop after verified completion or a documented closed reason
- Keep ecommerce, order, inventory, and product-information platforms as the system of record; only necessary coordination data belongs here

Before adding automation, run the template manually for a week. Remove ambiguous fields and confirm that two different users classify the same situation the same way. Consistency matters more than having a long form.
