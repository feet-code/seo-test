Examples make photography client proof selection and approval easier to design because they reveal where a neat diagram meets messy work. The scenarios below are not claims about a particular company; they are test cases independent photography studios and small commercial photo teams can run against a template or software trial.

### Scenario 1: A commercial client selects image 104 but references an old filename

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Client, shoot, and gallery, Proof-set version, Image identifier, then move it through publish the controlled proof set and collect image-specific selections and comments. If a proof deadline approaches without submitted selections, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason. ### Scenario 2: Two family members submit different album favorites

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Proof-set version, Image identifier, Selection status and intended output, then move it through publish the controlled proof set and collect image-specific selections and comments. If multiple reviewers provide conflicting decisions, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason. ### Scenario 3: A crop revision needs approval before a print order

Create the record before the first follow-up. Capture Image identifier, Selection status and intended output, Client comment, then move it through publish the controlled proof set and collect image-specific selections and comments. If a retouched revision or product choice changes the selected output, do not improvise in a private message; assign the exception, set a review date, and preserve the evidence needed for the next decision. Close with an explicit outcome and reason.

## Debrief each scenario

After running a scenario, ask:

- Did the record make every open photo proof decision needs one owner and a next review time?
- Did the record make completion requires recorded evidence that every client selection and approval identifies the exact images, revision, intended output, approver, and final decision?
- Did the record make automated reminders stop after verified completion or a documented closed reason?
- Did the record make keep the studio crm, contract, gallery, asset, and delivery platform as the system of record; only necessary coordination data belongs here?

Also check whether a new teammate could identify the owner, next action, and finish condition without opening another system.

## Convert scenarios into acceptance tests

Use the normal case, waiting case, and closed-without-completion case in every software demo. Require the vendor—or your own prototype—to show the full workflow rather than isolated feature screens. Export the resulting records and verify that the status history remains understandable.
