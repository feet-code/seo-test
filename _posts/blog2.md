Many process problems come from unclear ownership, repetitive manual work, or missing information.

## Find the bottleneck

Look for the step where work slows down or requires repeated manual effort.

## Make the change measurable

Define a simple outcome before changing the process so you can tell whether the new approach is better.

## Keep what works

Prefer small improvements that are easy to maintain over unnecessary complexity.
