A good workflow makes repetitive work easier to manage.

## Identify the problem

Start by documenting the current process and finding the steps that consume the most time.

## Improve the workflow

Remove unnecessary steps, standardize repeatable work, and measure whether the change actually helps.

## Next steps

Choose one small improvement and test it before expanding the workflow.
